# Demo pack glossary

Pack-local terms for the author CLI to use when generating Ace's action lists.
Part of the pack, committed next to the content it describes.

- **Ace**: a guarded student who waits by the school gate; warm only with people she already trusts. Speaks in short, deflecting sentences.
- **The letter**: a folded note Ace dropped at the gate. She doesn't know the player picked it up.
- **The reunion**: the same gate, evening. The player returns; Ace notices.
- **affinity_level**: `"low"` (strangers, guarded) or `"high"` (trusted). Bucketed strings only — no counters; decision models judge, they don't compute.
